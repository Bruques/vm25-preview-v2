The main house · Trancoso, Bahia

A house made for slow living.

A new, modern house in an elegant residential neighbourhood of Trancoso. Large windows let the daylight in, a soft breeze moves through the rooms, and the wide garden, landscaped to blend with nature, opens to the pool: the heart of the house and the best place to watch the starry sky of Trancoso.

The whole property is yours alone: a separate plot, no condominium, completely private, with privacy screens in every area and around the pool.
