The guest house · Trancoso, Bahia

Smaller, more secluded, just as warm.

An exclusive house in Trancoso, just a 3-minute walk from Praia dos Nativos. Surrounded by nature in a quiet area, it offers comfort, privacy and a prime location near the sea.

Modern tropical architecture blends natural materials, timber and wide glass doors, creating bright, airy, welcoming rooms. The highlight is the private infinity pool, perfect for unwinding in complete privacy. Ideal for couples, friends or small families.
